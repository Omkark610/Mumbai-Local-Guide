Top 6

Mumbai's Must-See Attractions

Six landmarks that capture the city, from its colonial waterfront to its busiest shopping lane.

Landmark Illustration

Gateway of India

Colaba, South Mumbai

Iconic waterfront monument and one of Mumbai's most recognizable landmarks, overlooking the Arabian Sea.

Good to know: Ferries to Elephanta Caves depart from the jetty right beside it. Best visited in the late afternoon.

Promenade Illustration

Marine Drive

Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose Rd

Scenic seaside promenade famous for its sunset views and glittering city skyline after dark.

Good to know: Nicknamed the "Queen's Necklace" for how its streetlights curve along the bay at night.

UNESCO Site Illustration

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus

Fort, South Mumbai

Historic UNESCO World Heritage railway station with stunning Victorian Gothic architecture.

Good to know: Still one of India's busiest stations — try to visit outside the morning rush.

Island Illustration

Elephanta Caves

Elephanta Island, Mumbai Harbour

Ancient rock-cut caves on Elephanta Island, home to remarkable sculptures carved over a thousand years ago.

Good to know: Reachable only by a short ferry ride from the Gateway of India. Plan for half a day.

Temple Illustration

Siddhivinayak Temple

Prabhadevi, Mumbai

One of Mumbai's most famous and visited temples, dedicated to Lord Ganesha and busy with devotees year-round.

Good to know: Expect long queues on Tuesdays, considered especially auspicious for this temple.

Sea Bridge Illustration

Mumbai Atal Setu

Mumbai Harbour, connecting Sewri to Nhava Sheva

India's longest sea bridge, linking South Mumbai to Navi Mumbai across the harbour and cutting travel time dramatically.

Good to know: Best experienced by car or cab; sunset drives across the bridge offer sweeping harbour views.
